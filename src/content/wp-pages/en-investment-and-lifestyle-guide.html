Welcome to the Mediterranean

Quality of Life and Opportunity in the Coastal Corridor

The coastal corridor linking Benicàssim (Castellón) with El Puig (Valencia) currently offers the best balance of quality of life, services, and cost along the entire Spanish Mediterranean coast.

It is the ideal destination for those seeking an active retirement, a holiday home, or a secure investment in an authentic setting, far removed from the overcrowding of other coastlines.

Strategic Connectivity

Europe within your reach

Accessibility is one of the greatest assets of this area.
You'll never be far from home.

Double Air Connection

We are located between two international airports.

Valencia Airport (VLC), with regular global connections, and Castellón Airport (CDT), which offers direct flights to major hubs in Central and Northern Europe.

Both are less than 40-50 minutes away by highway.

Direct Road Access

The AP-7 motorway (toll-free) connects our coast directly to the French border.

A comfortable and safe journey for those who prefer to travel in their own vehicle from Central Europe.

Destination Analysis

The Encounter of the Sun and Well-being

Benicàssim

The epicenter of Mediterranean "wellness". Famous for its microclimate, its historic stately villas, and a top-notch cultural and gastronomic scene.

Burriana y Moncofa

The essence of the sea. Villages with a Mediterranean soul offering apartments and villas at very competitive prices, quiet beaches and an unbeatable family atmosphere.

Canet d’en Berenguer

Home to Spain's most awarded beach. It offers modern infrastructure, nearby marinas, and a quality of services that makes it a residential gem.

Puerto de Sagunto

A dynamic city with its own hospital, large shopping centers, and a booming economy. It is the area with the greatest strategic appreciation potential.

El Puig

History and exclusivity just a stone's throw from Valencia city. The perfect place for those seeking an ideal retreat amidst orange groves and sea breezes, with the city only 15 minutes away.

Why invest here?

  • Health and Safety: Spain boasts one of the best healthcare systems in the world. Our area offers modern hospitals and exceptional public safety.
  • Efficient Cost of Living: Enjoy a premium standard of living (leisure, food, services) for a significantly lower investment than in your home country.
