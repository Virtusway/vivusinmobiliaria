Northern area of ​​Valencia

Greater Proximity to the Capital

This area offers the best connection to Valencia (airport and services) and is ideal for those looking for a second home with quick access to the city.

Canet d'En Berenguer

Considered one of the best beaches in Valencia (Bandera Azul). It stands out for its high-quality new developments.

Puerto de Sagunto

This area has great potential due to industrial regeneration (Volkswagen Gigafactory). It offers competitive prices and all the services of a vibrant city year-round, making it very attractive to the French market.

Puzol

Famous for its residential areas of villas and proximity to international schools, this is a quiet beach with protected dunes, ideal for families who prioritize privacy and nature.

El Puig

A charming coastal town with authentic character and wide, less crowded beaches. It combines local tradition with excellent connections to Valencia, perfect for a true immersion in the Spanish lifestyle.

La Pobla de Farnals

Its main attraction is the marina and yacht club. It boasts well-established residential areas and is a favorite choice for water sports enthusiasts and boat owners.

Downtown area

Tranquility and Nature

Ideal for those seeking total disconnection, more natural beaches and a slower pace of life.

Almardá

A semi-virgin beach with natural dune areas. It currently has a major coastal regeneration project that ensures long-term sustainability and appreciation of the investment.

Corinto

It offers a similar natural exclusivity to Almardá but with an even more tranquil atmosphere. A low-density development with dispersed homes, perfect for those seeking peace and quiet.

Almenara

It offers a similar natural exclusivity to Almardá but with an even more tranquil atmosphere. A low-density development with dispersed homes, perfect for those seeking peace and quiet.

Moncófar

A welcoming family summer resort town with an urban beach and all amenities. It stands out for its excellent value for money and consistent rental demand from local families.

Southern area of ​​Castellón

Connection and Services

Areas that combine beach with important urban infrastructure.

Burriana

A city steeped in history, with a vibrant marina and a well-equipped urban beach. It offers a festive and cultural atmosphere year-round, combining city life with the allure of the sea.
